- (note by user) Product: web app, AI CV & Job Application Assistant
- (note by user) Target users: students and young job seekers with limited experience writing professional CVs/applications
- (note by user) Core flow: user uploads existing CV (PDF/DOCX/TXT) + a job advertisement; AI analyzes both to tailor the application
- (note by user) Capabilities: (1) generate tailored cover letter, (2) analyze CV and suggest improvements, (3) extract key skills/keywords from job ad, (4) gap analysis comparing CV vs job requirements, (5) ATS-optimization suggestions, (6) user reviews/edits all AI output before use
- (note by user) Problem: students struggle to understand what employers want and how to adapt CV/application per job; writing a fresh application per posting is time-consuming
- (decision by user) V1 scope: focus on CV analysis + tailored applications only. Explicitly OUT of scope for v1: job tracking, automatic job search, interview prep, LinkedIn/job-board integrations
- (note by user) Context: IBE160 Programming with AI group project (Hogskolen i Molde), team = Sondre Skogheim + Kevin Heggdal
- (note by coach) Research: comparables are Teal, Rezi, Jobscan, Kickresume, Enhancv (AI tailoring/cover letters/ATS scoring/keyword-matching are all table-stakes, done by existing free/cheap tools)
- (insight by coach) No obvious feature-level moat vs. Teal/Jobscan/Rezi/Kickresume for the planned v1 scope; honest differentiation should be framed as execution/UX/target-niche (students specifically), not market novelty
- (note by coach) Research: known industry problem is AI fabricating metrics/numbers in generated bullets unless user supplies real data; over half of hiring managers penalize obviously-AI-written text; tailored cover letters correlate with real callback lifts (~21.8% vs ~13.1% generic)
- (decision by user) Stakes: this brief is the first mandatory graded IBE160 deliverable; must be generated in the group repo and pushed to GitHub by 2026-09-20

- (decision by user) Anti-hallucination requirement: system must not invent qualifications, skills, education, or work experience not present in the user's actual CV; all AI output is a reviewable/editable suggestion, never auto-submitted or assumed correct
